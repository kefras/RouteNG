A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before you hit the road.

What is RouteNG?＋

RouteNG connects drivers with spare seats to passengers travelling between Nigerian cities. You share the journey and contribute towards travel costs.

How do I book a seat?＋

Search your route and date, open a ride, and request your seats. Your booking is pending until the driver accepts it. Track its status in My trips.

How do payments work?＋

Your fare is shown before you request a seat. Booking does not charge you. Where online payments are enabled, you can pay from My trips after your driver accepts. Confirm arrangements with your driver before departure.

Can I cancel my booking?＋

Cancel a pending or accepted booking from My trips before departure. Let your driver know early. If you have already paid, contact support to arrange any applicable refund.
